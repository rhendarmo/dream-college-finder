# User Guide

## 1. Sign Up
Create an account with email and password.

## 2. Verify Email
Click the verification link sent to your email (in current state it will send the link in the console)

## 3. Log In
Log in using your credentials.

## 4. Create Profile
Enter:
- GPA
- SAT/ACT
- Major
- State preference

## 5. View Recommendations
You will see:
- Reach schools
- Target schools
- Safety schools

## 6. View School Details
Click a school to see:
- Admission stats
- Explanation
- Outcomes

## 7. Upload Resume
Upload a PDF resume for personalization.

## 8. Generate Advice
Get strategy recommendations based on your profile.

## 9. Use AI Assistant
Ask questions about schools and recommendations.
